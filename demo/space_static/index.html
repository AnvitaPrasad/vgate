Should you gate? (planner)

Per segment: help and harm (%), the better fixed policy and the paired labels that find it, oracle headroom and null_lead (what the best threshold of a score with no signal would lead by on a log of this size, in points); the help-vs-harm AUROC a gate needs to lead that policy by one point (auroc_floor: no score gets there below it; auroc_needed: a score of the usual shape, with its 5–95% range); with signal columns, the AUROC of a benefit gate fitted on the log (out of fold), its ceiling, estimate and lead, in points.
